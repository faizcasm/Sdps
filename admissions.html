THE ADMISSION JOURNEY

Four steps.
One new beginning.

Use this as a simple guide for your first conversation. The school office will confirm its current process and any forms or documents required.

Visit or enquire

Connect with the school office and ask about the class you are interested in.

Check the details

Confirm current seat availability, age criteria, school timings and fee information.

Prepare documents

Ask the school for its current document checklist before preparing or submitting anything.

Complete the process

Follow the instructions shared by the school and keep a note of the next steps.

No admission dates, fees, age cut-offs, required documents or seat availability have been published here because current official details could not be verified. Please obtain these directly from the school before applying.
