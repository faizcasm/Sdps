BEFORE YOU CALL

Questions worth
asking.

What should I ask about admissions? +

Ask about current class availability, eligibility, documents, fees, timings and the next step for your child's class.

What information should I bring? +

Ask the school office which documents are currently required before preparing or submitting anything.

Where can I confirm current details? +

The school office remains the best source for information that changes during the year.
