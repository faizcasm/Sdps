A WIDER KIND OF LEARNING

There is more than one way to grow.

A healthy school community gives learners space to discover their interests, practise cooperation and develop a sense of responsibility.

This page is a guide to the kinds of experiences families can ask the school about. Current clubs, activities, schedules and participation arrangements should be confirmed with the school office.

WAYS TO TAKE PART

Bring your
whole self.

Not every learner shines in the same place. Variety creates more doors into confidence and belonging.

01 / CREATE

Clubs & projects

Ask about opportunities to make, present, build, write, perform or investigate something that matters to you.

02 / MOVE

Play & activity

Movement, teamwork and healthy competition help learners develop energy, resilience and connection.

03 / CARE

Community spirit

Small acts of responsibility and kindness shape a school where everyone has room to participate.

04 / REFLECT

Wellbeing

Encouragement, listening and supportive routines help young people feel ready to learn.

Activity names, clubs and schedules have intentionally not been guessed. The school can replace this guide with its approved programme, calendar and student handbook before launch.
