# School content approval checklist

Before this concept website is published as the school's official website, obtain approval and confirm these items with the school management.

## Identity and governance

- [ ] Confirm the official registered school name and preferred styling of “Lal-e-Arifa Memorial”.
- [ ] Obtain the official school logo / crest and approval to use it.
- [ ] Confirm the principal / administrator name and whether it should appear publicly.
- [ ] Confirm the school's board affiliation and any registration or recognition details that may be published.
- [ ] Confirm the current classes offered, including whether pre-primary is open.

## Contact and admissions

- [ ] Confirm the public telephone number and WhatsApp number.
- [ ] Confirm the official email address and who monitors it.
- [ ] Confirm school office hours and holiday / closure arrangements.
- [ ] Confirm admission dates, eligibility, documents, fee schedule and seat availability.
- [ ] Confirm whether a form, online enquiry workflow or prospectus download is needed.

## Campus and learning

- [ ] Replace all illustrative stock photographs with approved photos of this school's building, classrooms, library, playground and school activities.
- [ ] Obtain parent / guardian consent for identifiable student photographs.
- [ ] Confirm current facilities and accessibility information.
- [ ] Confirm the school's description, educational values and any official mission / vision statement.
- [ ] Confirm transport, safety, medical and extracurricular information before publishing.

## Launch checks

- [ ] Confirm exact map pin and address wording with the school office.
- [ ] Approve all website copy, photos and the footer developer credit.
- [ ] Connect the school's chosen domain.
- [ ] Run a production build and test all links on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Submit the live sitemap to Google Search Console after launch.
