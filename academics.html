LEARNING, STEP BY STEP

Every stage is a new beginning.

Children grow in different ways and at different speeds. Across the school years, they need strong foundations, opportunities to practise, and the encouragement to take on new challenges.

These learning stages help families understand the class range reflected in public listings. They are not a substitute for the school's current syllabus, subject list or assessment plan.

01 / CLASSES I–V

Primary years

Early school years build the foundations for communication, reading, numeracy, observation and learning with others.

02 / CLASSES VI–VIII

Middle years

Learners make connections across subjects, develop independent study habits and begin to explore ideas in greater depth.

03 / CLASSES IX–X

Secondary years

Subject knowledge, practice and self-discipline become increasingly important as learners prepare for their next academic step.
