A SIMPLE PUBLISHING RHYTHM

Useful first.
Current always.

A good school news page helps families answer one question quickly: is there anything I need to know today?

01

Clear dates

Lead with the day, time and audience so important information is easy to scan.

02

Official sources

Publish only school-approved notices and link families to the office for questions.

03

Warm stories

Balance reminders with the everyday moments that make a school community feel real.
