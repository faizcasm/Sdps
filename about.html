THE STORY OF SPRINGDALES

Rooted in place.
Open to possibility.

Lal-e-Arifa Memorial Springdales Public School is part of the Palhallan community, with a story that began in 2004 and a purpose that looks towards the generations ahead.

A SCHOOL IN PALHALLAN

A meaningful education begins with belonging.

A school is more than a building or a timetable. It is a place where young people learn to express an idea, listen to another point of view, build a skill and discover what matters to them.

Springdales Public School carries the name Lal-e-Arifa Memorial and is located in Palhallan, Pattan, Baramulla. Public school-directory records identify the school as established in 2004, co-educational and serving classes I to X.

This website introduces the school and helps families find their way to the campus. The school itself remains the best source for current information about its programmes, policies and daily routines.

WHAT WE VALUE

Good learning.
Good people.

The values below describe the educational spirit this website is designed to communicate. They are not presented as a formally adopted school policy.

01 / CURIOSITY

Keep asking why.

Make room for questions, thoughtful exploration and the confidence to learn something new.

02 / CHARACTER

Grow with care.

Respect, kindness and responsibility make a stronger learning community.

03 / POSSIBILITY

Find your way.

Recognise individual strengths and encourage every learner to keep moving forward.

School details on this page are based on publicly available directory records. The school administration should review and approve all institutional statements, policies and official imagery before this website is published as the school official site.
