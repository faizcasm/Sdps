08 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A clearer start
for every family.

These answers share what is publicly available and point you to the school office for anything that changes year to year.

Which classes does the school serve? +

Public school-directory records list classes I to X. Please confirm current class availability directly with the school office.

Where is Springdales Public School located? +

The school is in Palhallan, Pattan, Baramulla, Jammu & Kashmir. Use the contact page to open directions in Google Maps.

How do I enquire about admission? +

Start with the admissions guide, then contact or visit the school to confirm eligibility, seats, documents, fees and timings for the current year.

Can I visit the campus? +

Yes—families are encouraged to speak with the school office first so the team can share the best time and current visitor arrangements.
