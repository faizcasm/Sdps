01 / OUR SCHOOL

A legacy of learning in Palhallan

Every learner has a story.
Every story deserves a beginning.

Lal-e-Arifa Memorial Springdales Public School is part of the Palhallan community. Since 2004, its name has stood for the promise of education and the possibilities it can create for young people.

We believe a meaningful school experience should make space for curiosity, kindness, discipline and the confidence to take the next step. Those values shape the way we think about learning, inside and beyond the classroom.

“Education is not simply about reaching a destination. It is about discovering what you are capable of along the way.”

Every child's learning journey is different. The school office can guide families through available classes, admission requirements and the next steps.

  1. Start a conversation

    Visit the school office to ask about admissions and the class you are interested in.

  2. Understand the requirements

    Confirm seat availability, age criteria, documents and the current fee structure.

  3. Complete the application

    Collect the school's current form and follow the instructions shared by the office.

    Check the school calendar, timings and any joining guidance before classes begin.
